# Elbow: what Magee teaches and what a vision model can check (2026-09-28)

Agent 3 (CV / biomechanics research). Condensed; sources in the transcript.

## Headline
1. The discriminating variable is FRONTAL-PLANE lateral deviation, not the
   sagittal 90-degree "L". Cabarkapa et al. 2021 (CEJSSM 36(4)): forearm angle
   from vertical 7.2 deg (made) vs 10.7 (missed) within proficient shooters,
   p=0.004 - the only significant variable; proficient 7.9 vs non-proficient
   19.8. Elbow FLEXION was tighter in proficient shooters (72 vs 80), so a
   sub-90 elbow is not a fault. A grader that checks "is it 90?" finds every
   shooter close enough and defaults high.
2. Magee diagnoses the elbow through the HAND. NABC 2015 clinic: "The elbow
   must be straight and under the basketball. The forearm and upper arm should
   create an 'L' shape rather than a 'V'." "On the shooting hand, you should
   not see your pinky on the side of the ball. If you see your pinky, your
   elbow is sticking out." On Ben Simmons: "his left elbow is out ... he
   shoots it a little bit across his body."
3. Coaches describe the CATAPULT as a ball-PATH fault visible in the gather
   and set frames: ball travels up AND backward toward/behind the head, STOPS,
   then changes direction (Penny: "START / STOP / START"); both elbows high
   and wide; upper arm behind vertical; forearm points backward; flat sling.
   NOT visible at release, which is where the flag has been looking.

## Visible cues by phase (front / three-quarter camera)
Gather: elbow away from the ribs ("throwing vs shooting"); both elbows out.
Set point: elbow outside the vertical line through the ball; forearm tilted
  (Cabarkapa: <=8 good, ~11 marginal, ~20 bad); ball BESIDE the head rather
  than in front of/above the forehead on the shooting side; ball over the
  midline/crown forces the elbow out; pinky visible on the side of the ball.
Release: elbow pointing out not down; arm crossing the face/midline.
Follow-through: hand finishing off to the side; guide hand thrown out.

## Klay Thompson reference at set point
Ball at forehead level, closer to the right side of the face, in front of the
head; elbow about shoulder height and on the shoulder's vertical; forearm
within ~10 deg of vertical; reaches the set point before the feet leave the
floor; guide hand flat on the side.

## Severity ladder (sourced)
9-10 elbow under the ball on its vertical line, forearm vertical, ball above
     the forehead on the shooting side, pinky hidden.
7-8  elbow slightly outside the ball line, forearm 10-20 deg off vertical,
     ball still in front of the head, one continuous path.
5-6  elbow clearly outside the ball line; pinky visible; hand on the side of
     the ball; follow-through drifts off-line.
3-4  ball beside or across the face; forearm tilted so the hand is inboard and
     the elbow outboard (a V); arm swings across the body; upper arm off the ribs.
1-2  catapult: ball travels behind/over the head and stops; shooting elbow
     above the ear; both elbows high and wide; flat sling; two-hand push.
